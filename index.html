How to diagnose homepage load errors or other site-specific errors (not project errors)

  1. Open Chrome. Download it if necessary.
  2. Press Ctrl+Shift+C (or right-click → Inspect) to open DevTools.
  3. Ensure the “Console” tab is active. It should resemble the first screenshot.
  4. Start screen recording.
  5. Go to websim and let the console log fill up if it happens.
  6. From DevTools, switch to the “Network” tab (second screenshot).
  7. You may need to resize the Network panel; it should resemble the third screenshot.
  8. Refresh the WebSim tab.
  9. DM me the video, or email it to support@websim.ai.

Tip: Use this recorder to capture just the WebSim tab with its audio, then attach the resulting ZIP.
